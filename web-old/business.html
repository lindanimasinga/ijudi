    Business Programmes

    Partner with iZinga.
    Grow your business.

    Whether you refer clients or build iZinga's presence in your community, we have a programme designed for you.

    Referral Partner Programme

    Refer furniture retailers, estate agents, or businesses to iZinga and earn commission when they start delivering with us. This programme is built for sales professionals, logistics consultants, and well-connected business people who know companies that need reliable last-mile delivery across South Africa. There is no delivery work involved — you make the introduction, we handle the rest.

    • Earn commissions on qualifying referrals you bring in
    • Three referral paths: customer referrals, furniture or parcel clients, and food store partners
    • Dedicated partner onboarding and support
    • Track your referrals and earnings in your partner dashboard

    Programme terms are currently under legal review. Registering now expresses interest only — no agreement is active until terms are finalised and you accept them separately.

    Ambassador Programme

    Help build iZinga's driver network on the ground. As an iZinga Ambassador, you identify prospective drivers and messengers in your community, walk them through the registration process, and assist them in uploading the required documents — then earn commission for every driver who is successfully approved. This programme is for community connectors, field agents, and motivated individuals who want to grow iZinga's presence where they live.

    • Recruit and onboard new iZinga drivers in your area
    • Support drivers through the registration and document upload process
    • Earn commission for every driver you successfully introduce to iZinga
    • Track your referred drivers via your Ambassador dashboard

    Programme terms are currently under legal review. Registering now expresses interest only — no agreement is active until terms are finalised and you accept them separately.

    By registering, you will receive the full programme terms and earn structure for your review before any agreement is active.
